    <p class="eyebrow">01</p>
    <h2>Quantum materials</h2>
    <p>We study correlated electronic systems in which collective behavior gives rise to phases and excitations that cannot be understood from individual particles alone.</p>
    <p>Our work focuses on connecting local measurements to broader questions about superconductivity, topology, and emergent electronic structure.</p>

  <div class="research-detail__copy">
    <p class="eyebrow">02</p>
    <h2>Quantum sensing</h2>
    <p>Precision measurements can expose weak signals that are hidden in conventional experiments. We develop methods that resolve electronic behavior with high spatial and energy resolution.</p>
    <p>These approaches help us test microscopic theories directly and distinguish competing physical mechanisms.</p>
  </div>
</section>
